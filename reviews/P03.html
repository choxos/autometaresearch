Independent adversarial review of P03. Verdict: blocked.
Published

September 19, 2026

Review of Is bad news cited more?.

ImportantVerdict: blocked

A reviewer produced a fatal finding backed by a verbatim quote and no reviewer rebutted it. Under the rule, evidence outranks votes, so this outcome does not depend on the tally.

Adjudicated by build/audit/adjudicate.py at rule version 1. Coverage is complete: chatgpt, grok returned a verdict out of a roster of chatgpt, grok.

1 fatal, 17 major, 9 minor. 18 of the severe findings carry a verbatim quote.

What each reviewer said

chatgpt

Read-only checks reproduced the citation summaries for the 1,790 stored rows, but uncovered duplicate article identities, version mismatches, and a false recent-publication percentage. The classifier gate can pass despite complete failure to identify adequate conclusions. The proposed model also omits the promised journal adjustment, and the falsification rule misinterprets confidence intervals. These are repairable protocol defects. Classification and detectable-effect estimates remain unavailable, as disclosed. Literature searches found related precedents, but no verified study testing this exact within-meta-research contrast.

grok

Citation counts in section 5 match data/lit/openalex-records-summary.json and studies/_shared/openalex_probe.py: median 8, mean 36.29, 90th percentile 75, maximum 2937, 284 zeros, 1790 of 1805 PMIDs. The frozen snapshot exists. Direction has not been crossed with citations, so this is not a look at the hypothesis of the P01v2 kind. The kappa stop is written before any labels. Those parts hold. The primary analysis does not. Section 2’s single incidence rate ratio cannot be read from a four-level direction variable whose mixed and not-applicable levels have no stated fate in the glm.nb. topic_block and journal_size are not defined, and 1009 of 1830 labelled records carry two or more topic labels. The 76 percent figure used to keep 2025 and 2026 papers is the 2020-onward share (1375 of 1805 PMIDs), not the 2025-2026 share (484 of 1805, 26.8 percent). Of 284 zeros, 221 are from 2025-2026, so most zeros are papers with almost no time at risk, not papers nobody used. The citation tail that motivates quantile regression includes phrase-matched methods reviews, science-of-science surveys, and a clinical meta-analysis, not only verdicts that a practice is inadequate. I did not find a published study that already runs this exact contrast on meta-research articles. Duyx et al. 2017 (PMID 28603008) is the citation-bias review this question belongs to and is not cited. Serra-Garcia and Gneezy 2021 (PMID 34020944) is related and studies a different population.

Fatal findings

F1. The primary null is a single incidence rate ratio for conclusion direction. The exposure instrument in section 6 has fou

Reviewer grok · Section 2. Hypothesis

The primary null is a single incidence rate ratio for conclusion direction. The exposure instrument in section 6 has four labels (inadequate, adequate, mixed, not applicable) and section 7 enters direction as one term in glm.nb. A four-level factor in that model yields three coefficients, not one IRR. Mixed and not applicable have no stated coding, reference level, or exclusion rule. Not applicable is not a spare bin: the unscreened tier A query admits methods papers, science-of-science reviews, and clinical articles that only use a query phrase, so a large share of the 1790 rows may be not applicable. Until those rows are dropped or separately modeled, the coefficient named in section 2 does not exist, and probe PC cannot compute a detectable rate ratio for the contrast the hypothesis actually states.

The incidence rate ratio for conclusion direction is 1.

What would fix it. Define the primary estimand as the negative-binomial IRR for inadequate versus adequate. Exclude mixed and not applicable from that model and report their counts. Name the reference level. If mixed is retained, specify the dummy coding and which coefficient is the primary test. Recompute PC on the inadequate-versus-adequate split only, after the 300 labels exist.

Major findings

M1. Overall four-category kappa does not validate the primary inadequate-versus-adequate comparison. Computed counterexample

Reviewer chatgpt · Section 6. Exposure, and the gate

Overall four-category kappa does not validate the primary inadequate-versus-adequate comparison. Computed counterexample: among 300 reference labels, let 210 be inadequate, 30 adequate, 30 mixed, and 30 not applicable. A classifier that labels every adequate article inadequate and classifies everything else correctly achieves kappa 0.762, exceeding the gate despite zero sensitivity for adequate conclusions. A single overall misclassification rate also cannot characterize these asymmetric errors.

If neither model reaches kappa 0.70 against the author’s labels, the study stops and the negative methodological result is published.

What would fix it. Require prespecified class-specific sensitivity and precision for adequate and inadequate conclusions, with uncertainty and sufficient validation examples in both classes. Publish the complete confusion matrix, account for stratified sampling, and propagate category-specific classification uncertainty into the analysis.

M2. The reference standard lacks an operational coding rubric. No rule distinguishes negative background rhetoric from the f

Reviewer chatgpt · Section 6. Exposure, and the gate

The reference standard lacks an operational coding rubric. No rule distinguishes negative background rhetoric from the focal conclusion, improvement from adequate absolute practice, or multiple conflicting practice assessments from one overall verdict. Author-model agreement on the same abstract measures agreement with one person’s interpretation, not whether the instrument recovers the article’s conclusion. Blinding to citation counts is also unspecified.

The exposure is the direction of the article’s own conclusion, and no rule extracts it from an abstract.

What would fix it. Freeze a coding manual with decision rules and examples before labeling. Blind reference coding to citation outcomes, independently double-code and adjudicate a validation subset, and check abstract labels against full text. Alternatively, explicitly restrict the exposure and claims to evaluative framing in abstracts. Freeze model versions, prompts, and the primary-classifier selection rule before validation.

With right-skewed citations the mean is already tail-sensitive (mean 36.29 versus median 8). M14. What would falsify the hypothesis is an interval containing 1. No interval, alpha, or smallest interesting rate ratio is

Reviewer grok · Section 2. Hypothesis

What would falsify the hypothesis is an interval containing 1. No interval, alpha, or smallest interesting rate ratio is named. Probe PC, which would say what rate ratio the realized split can detect, is outstanding and depends on the exposure split that does not yet exist. M15. The protocol frames the question as publication bias moved to the citation stage and does not cite the citation-bias lit

Reviewer grok · Section 1. The question

The protocol frames the question as publication bias moved to the citation stage and does not cite the citation-bias literature. Duyx, Urlings, Swaen, Bouter, and Zeegers, Journal of Clinical Epidemiology 2017, PMID 28603008, reviewed 52 studies and found statistically significant articles cited 1.6 times as often (95 percent CI 1.3 to 1.8) and articles whose authors concluded support for their hypothesis cited 2.7 times as often (CI 2.0 to 3.7). Jannot, Agoritsas, Gayet-Ageron, and Perneger, Journal of Clinical Epidemiology 2013, PMID 23257169, found significant studies cited about twice as often, with much of the association running through journal impact factor. That literature says positive and hypothesis-supporting results are cited more, not less. In meta-research, concluding that a practice is inadequate is often the result the authors sought, so inadequate may be the field’s positive finding. If so, P03 is a population-specific test of ordinary citation bias, not an inverted publication-bias mechanism. Serra-Garcia and Gneezy, Science Advances 2021, PMID 34020944, found nonreplicable original papers cited more than replicable ones; that is a different population (original findings, not meta-research conclusions) and is also uncited. I did not find a study that already classifies meta-research conclusions as inadequate versus adequate and models OpenAlex or Web of Science counts on that split.

This is publication bias asked about the field that studies publication bias, and asked at the citation stage rather than at acceptance.

What would fix it. Cite Duyx 2017 and Jannot 2013 as citation bias, not publication bias at the citation stage. State whether inadequate is coded as bad news or as support for a problem-finding hypothesis. Distinguish Serra-Garcia and Gneezy 2021 as related work on a different unit. Keep the claim of novelty only for the meta-research-conclusion contrast, which I could not find already done.

M16. The reference standard is 300 author labels on abstracts. Three gaps make that instrument fail to measure the exposure i

Reviewer grok · Section 6. Exposure, and the gate

The reference standard is 300 author labels on abstracts. Three gaps make that instrument fail to measure the exposure in section 2 for the sample in section 4. First, 112 of 1805 PMID records have no abstract (108 of the 1790 OpenAlex matches). Classification of abstracts cannot be extended to those rows, and the protocol never mentions them. Second, stratified is unspecified: not by year, topic, journal, citation quartile, or publication type. A validation sample that is light on the citation tail or on not-applicable methods papers will not estimate the error that matters for section 5. Third, the author wrote the hypothesis that bad news is cited more and is not blinded to fame or to the frozen citation file. Highly cited phrase matches (Deeks 2003, Wood 2008, Smaldino 2016, Fortunato 2018) are recognizable. Outcome-contaminated gold labels will inflate kappa and bake the hypothesis into the instrument. Kappa 0.70 on four classes can also be carried by agreement on not applicable while inadequate versus adequate, the actual contrast, remains noisy.

A stratified random sample of 300 abstracts is classified by the author into inadequate, adequate, mixed, or not applicable.

What would fix it. Restrict the analysis set to records with an abstract, or specify a full-text rule for the rest, and recompute section 5 on that set. Pre-specify the stratification factors and include citation-quartile and publication-type strata. Blind the author to citation counts, journal, year, and title when labelling, presenting abstract text only. Report kappa for the binary inadequate-versus-adequate contrast among applicable records, not only the four-class kappa, and gate on that binary agreement.
