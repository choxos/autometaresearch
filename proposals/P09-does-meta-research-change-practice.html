Does meta-research change the practice it documents?

design
waste
impact
The question the field exists to answer about itself. Design only; the unit of analysis needs the author.
Published

September 16, 2026

ImportantStatus: design only. No probe has run. This is the one that most needs the author’s judgment.

Every other document here can have its feasibility settled by a script. This one cannot, because its unit of analysis is “a documented problem” and enumerating those is a judgment about which meta-research papers were consequential enough to have plausibly changed anything.

Published at design stage deliberately. It is the most important question in the set and pretending otherwise by deferring it would be a choice about what is easy rather than what matters.

1. The question

Meta-research documents problems on the assumption that documenting them helps. That assumption is rarely tested, and the field that insists on evidence for other people’s interventions has little for its own.

2. Hypothesis

Publication of a meta-research study documenting poor practice in a defined population is followed by improvement in that practice, beyond the secular trend.

Primary null. No change in level or slope at the publication date, beyond trend.

What would falsify it. No discontinuity and no slope change. Given how many such studies exist and how little practice appears to move, the null is the more likely outcome and the design must be able to report it credibly. That is why it is an interrupted time series with a control series rather than a before-and-after comparison.

3. Design

Controlled interrupted time series. For each documented problem:

  • Intervention: publication of the meta-research study.
  • Series: the practice it measured, re-measured by us at regular intervals before and after, in the same population the study defined.
  • Control series: the same practice over the same period in a comparable population the study did not examine.

The control series is what separates a real effect from the secular improvement that affects everything. A study reporting improvement without one is measuring the calendar.

4. Sample, and what the author must decide

The unit is a documented problem. A candidate qualifies when the original study:

  1. defines its population explicitly enough to reconstruct it from a query,
  2. reports a rate with a denominator,
  3. was published at least three years ago, so there is post-period to observe,
  4. measures something re-measurable from metadata or full text without human reading at scale.

Criterion 4 is the binding one. “Adherence to CONSORT item 6a” is not re-measurable at scale; “carries a data availability statement”, “reports a registration number”, “is registered prospectively” are.

The author selects the candidate problems. A model proposing which papers were influential would be choosing the intervention to suit the hypothesis, and there is no defensible automatic criterion. Five to ten problems is the target.

5. Analysis

In R. Segmented regression per problem, with a control series and autocorrelation handled explicitly.

gls(rate ~ time + level + trend + series + series:level + series:trend,
    correlation = corARMA(p = 1), data = d)

Pooled across problems by random-effects meta-analysis of the level and slope changes, since a single problem is a case study and the question is about the field.

6. Threats, and what happened to each

threat what was done removed or disclosed
Secular trend mistaken for effect Control series; the primary estimate is the difference in differences Removed
Autocorrelation inflating significance Modeled explicitly, order selected and reported Removed
Selection of influential papers The author selects, against written criteria, and the criteria are published before selection Disclosed
Co-interventions A journal policy change often follows the same concern that prompted the study; the design cannot separate them, and this is the central limitation Disclosed
Regression to the mean A study is often published because a rate looked bad; the rate would drift up without any effect Disclosed, and the control series partly addresses it
Re-measurement differs from the original measurement Our instrument is re-applied to the pre-period as well, so the comparison is internally consistent even where it disagrees with the original paper’s number Removed

7. Probes

probe computes goes to what would change the design status
PA candidate problems meeting criteria 1 to 4 4 fewer than 5 makes pooling meaningless outstanding, needs the author
PB for each candidate, whether the population is reconstructible as a PubMed query 4 a population that cannot be reconstructed cannot be a series outstanding
PC pre-period length and number of points per series 5 fewer than about 8 pre-period points makes segmented regression unreliable outstanding, depends on PB
PD agreement between our re-measurement and the original study’s reported rate 6 a large gap means the instrument differs and the series is not the one the paper measured outstanding

Deviations

Recorded on the result page: what changed, when, why, and the estimate before and after where that can be computed. A deviation is not a failure; concealing one is.

What this cannot show

  • It cannot attribute change to the paper rather than to the concern that produced the paper. This is the central limitation and no observational design removes it.
  • It cannot detect an effect on practice that is not recorded in metadata or full text.
  • A null result would not show that meta-research is useless, only that this particular route from documentation to practice is not visible at this resolution.
